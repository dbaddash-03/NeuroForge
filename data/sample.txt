NeuroForge is a tiny laboratory for understanding language models.
A language model predicts the next token from the tokens that came before it.
Embeddings turn discrete symbols into vectors.
Attention lets every position gather information from earlier positions.
A Transformer block combines attention, normalization, residual connections, and a feed-forward network.
Stacking blocks creates a deeper representation of context.
Training changes the model parameters to reduce next-token prediction loss.
Generation repeatedly samples the next token and appends it to the sequence.
This small system is educational rather than production scale.
The goal is to make the invisible machinery of an LLM visible.
